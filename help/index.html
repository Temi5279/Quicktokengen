About QuickTokenGen Pro

QuickTokenGen Pro is a light-weight, privacy-focused developer suite designed for quick, secure utilities. We prioritize client-side execution, meaning your sensitive passphrases, payloads, and strings are computed directly inside your browser rather than sent to remote servers.

Tool Usage Guides

  • Password Generator: Select your desired password length and character sets (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special symbols) to create cryptographic passphrases with instant strength feedback.
  • QR Code Creator: Enter any text or URL to generate instant, downloadable PNG QR codes for sharing links, contact details, or credentials.
  • Base64 Encoder & Decoder: Convert raw strings into valid Base64 ASCII output or decode encrypted payloads back into human-readable text.
  • Text Repeater: Instantly duplicate text strings or emojis up to 5,000 times for testing line lengths, input boundaries, or form fields.
