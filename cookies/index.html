Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how QuickTokenGen uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners in order to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

2. How We Use Cookies

We do not utilize proprietary tracking cookies to profile or track your personal browsing habits. However, our third-party advertising partners and analytics providers may place cookies on your device to serve targeted advertisements based on your visits to this and other websites across the internet.

3. Managing Cookies

You can choose to disable or selectively turn off our cookies or third-party cookies through your browser settings. However, this may affect how you interact with our website or third-party services.
